About Our Company

The Story Behind Bingo Tour & Travels

Founded and managed directly by Owner Jyotiram with one core mission: To provide safe, honest, and truly hospitable road travel experiences across India.

A Passion for Hospitality & Safe Driving

After years of professional chauffeuring serving families, corporate executives, event delegations, and international travelers, Jyotiram (Owner) established Bingo Tour & Travels in Sanganer, Jaipur. Having driven across every corner of India—from the rugged trails of the Himalayas (Kedarnath Dham, Badrinath, Shimla, Manali) to the cultural heritage hubs of Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan—Jyotiram understands that a successful road trip is built on peace of mind, punctuality, and transparent communication.

Offering a versatile fleet of comfortable taxis including Swift Dzire, Toyota Innova, and Bus / Tempo Traveller ensures that whether you are planning a solo one-way trip to Delhi/Agra, a large corporate conference transport, or a multi-day family pilgrimage, you receive uncompromised vehicle hygiene, polite owner support, and no hidden charges.

Our Guiding Principles

  • Passenger Safety First: Disciplined driving adhering to speed limits across mountain highways and busy national corridors.
  • Personalized Owner Care: Direct coordination with Owner Jyotiram without middlemen or agency markups.
  • Strict Punctuality: Guaranteed on-time pickups for airport transfers, corporate conferences, and early morning temple departures.
