About Our Company

The Story Behind Bingo Tour & Travels

Founded by Jyotiram with one core mission: To provide safe, honest, and truly hospitable road travel experiences across Rajasthan and India.

A Passion for Hospitality & Safe Driving

After years of professional driving experience serving families, corporate executives, and international travelers, Jyotiram established Bingo Tour & Travels in Sanganer, Jaipur. Having driven across every corner of India—from the rugged, snow-capped trails of the Himalayas (Kedarnath Dham & Amarnath Yatra) to the sweeping sand dunes of Jaisalmer—Jyotiram understands that a successful road trip isn't just about getting from point A to point B; it’s about peace of mind, punctuality, and warm hospitality.

Operating his own vehicle—a meticulously maintained Toyota Innova 2.5 GX (Reg No: RJ 14 UE 1517)—ensures that every client gets uncompromised vehicle hygiene, functioning dual air-conditioning, polite demeanor, and transparent billing without middlemen markups.

Our Guiding Principles

  • Passenger Safety First: Calm, disciplined driving adhering to speed limits, especially through treacherous mountain passes and busy highways.
  • Atithi Devo Bhava: Treating every passenger with utmost respect and extending assistance with elderly travelers and children during long temple darshan queues.
  • Strict Punctuality: Arriving 15 minutes ahead of scheduled airport, railway station, or hotel departure timings.
  • 100% Legal & Verified: Clean paperwork, government RTO commercial registration, and regular vehicle fitness inspections.
