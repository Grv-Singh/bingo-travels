Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, fare calculation, and travel guarantees.

help Are toll taxes and parking charges included in the fare?

For outstation and per-km bookings, toll taxes, state border entry taxes, and parking fees are charged transparently at actual toll slips. For dedicated one-way packages, all-inclusive packages can be customized directly on inquiry.

help How is outstation per-day distance calculated?

Outstation bookings have a standard daily minimum of 250 km (or 300 km for Tempo Travellers) across North India. If your multi-day journey totals 800 km across 3 days (minimum 750 km), you pay only for the 800 km driven.

help Can we book one-way cabs without paying round-trip fare?

Yes! We offer dedicated one-way drops from Jaipur to Delhi, Agra, Ajmer, Udaipur, and Kota. You only pay for the one-way drop without having to pay for the vehicle's return journey.

help How do I confirm my booking with Bingo Travels?

Simply send your journey dates and vehicle choice via our instant inquiry form or call +91 80589 85804. We will share the driver's contact details and vehicle plate number prior to your scheduled pickup time.
